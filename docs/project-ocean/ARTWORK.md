> Use case: ads-marketing
> Asset type: a premium ultra-wide 1920:720 homepage carousel marketing artwork for Project Ocean, a browser-based modular 3D character creation and wardrobe tool.
> Primary request: Turn the actual angular modular character design language in the provided app screenshot references into polished campaign key art. This is concept artwork, not a screenshot or literal UI.
> Input images: Image 1 current-v02-preview.jpg: reference for Rookie, the tall thin rigid low-poly figure with a small simple cuboid head, lavender swept mohawk, black shirt, lavender accents and long straight angular limbs. Image 2 river-expanded-basics-preview.png: reference for the same character wearing swappable hoodie, cargo trousers, high-top sneakers and hat.
> Scene: a sophisticated charcoal digital character workshop with a very subtle perspective construction grid. Center-right, show one fully assembled Rookie character in three-quarter view, confident relaxed stance, recognizably angular rigid geometric pieces. Around and behind the figure, arrange a small number of separated matching wardrobe parts, alternate geometric hair and hat options, and a ghosted skeletal assembly study, like a beautiful exploded-view design exhibit. Two smaller outfit variations recede to the right, making the character-building purpose immediately legible.
> Style: high-end art-directed stylized 3D render, crisp planar geometry, matte painted surfaces, beautiful soft contact shadows and refined editorial composition. Preserve the slender proportions and simple blank cuboid facial design from references. The finish can be more beautiful than the app while remaining recognizable. Color language: charcoal, soft warm stone, muted lavender and dusty rose from the actual Ocean tools, with restrained cool rim light.
> Composition: panoramic 8:3, full character visible with headroom, key subjects centered within the middle 60% so a center mobile crop retains the hero. Left lower quarter deliberately quiet and dark for the website's existing white title and button overlay. Strong visual hierarchy and layered depth, little clutter.
> Constraints: no typography, no words, no letters, no logos or watermarks, no fake UI, no literal ocean water, sailboats or marine life, no round toy heads, no photorealistic humans. This should communicate shaping a character and dressing variations.
